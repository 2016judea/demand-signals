Help Wanted, Decoded

What US employers say they need right now, read from the postings themselves. Pick a sector to see its needs, then check how much the region changes them.

Across every sector

  • Hours

    The low-wage half needs your availability more than your skills. 63% of retail postings are part-time and 50% ask for weekends. Senior care is 62% part-time and restaurants 56%.

  • Licences

    A credential is the gate in almost every hands-on job. CDL in 38% of transportation postings, a pharmacy licence or PTCB in 32% of pharma, BLS/CPR in 32% of healthcare, a real-estate licence in 27%, a CPA in 27% of finance, a guard card in 25% of security, journeyman/master in 20% of trades.

  • AI

    AI has moved into the job description. 55% of data roles and 27% of software roles mention AI or LLM tools. So do 10% of marketing, 9% of finance and 7% of legal, HR and engineering postings.

  • Training

    Employers will train rather than wait. Paid training or tuition appears in 45% of security and policing postings, 30% of automotive, 28% of senior care and 26% of transportation.

  • Region

    Region changes the price and the paperwork, not the job.

How this was built

A "need" is a requirement or offer the posting states: a licence, a tool, a shift, a bonus. Each percentage is the share of a sector's postings whose text matches it. Phrase shares such as "early childhood education" are counted once per employer, so one large company's boilerplate cannot inflate them. Region comes from the posting's own state.

Indeed returns the 50 most relevant postings per search and metro, so sector sizes here reflect the sampling plan rather than the real mix of openings. JOLTS gives the real scale. Energy (275) and agriculture (382) are small samples after removing off-topic matches such as line cooks returned for "lineman".
